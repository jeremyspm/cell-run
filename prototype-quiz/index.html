Cell Run · Level 1: The Heart

You're a red blood cell. Get through the heart without a wrong turn.

What is this?

A study game for the route blood takes through the heart. At each step you pick where to go. If you take a wrong turn, a closed valve stops you and tells you why. Gates ask exam-style questions. At the end a disease "boss" blocks the exit, and you beat it by putting its cause-and-effect chain in order.

It takes about 5 minutes. It works offline and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
